Official Ludo Rules

1. Starting the Race: Roll a 6 to move any token out of your colored home triangle onto your starting square.

2. Bonus Turns: Rolling a 6 awards an extra roll. Rolling three 6s in a row forfeits the turn.

3. Capturing Opponents: Landing on a non-safe square occupied by an opponent's token sends their token back to their starting yard and grants you a bonus roll.

4. Safe Squares (Stars): Squares marked with a star or colored starting squares are safe zones where no token can be captured.

5. Reaching Home: Complete one full circuit around the board and enter your colored home stretch. An exact roll is required to enter the center home triangle.

How to Play

Step 1: Choose Play with AI, Play with Friends, or Play Online.

Step 2: Select 2 to 6 players. Choosing 5 players automatically loads the Pentagon Board, and 6 players loads the Hexagon Board.

Step 3: Tap the glowing 3D dice inside your player card when it is your turn, then tap any highlighted token to move.

Winning Strategy Guides

Spread Your Tokens: Bring out at least two tokens early so you always have a safe move when rolling low numbers.

Control Star Zones: Park tokens on star squares right ahead of opponent home yards to pressure their newly spawned pieces.

Pentagon & Hexagon Tactics: On 5 and 6-player boards, the outer track is longer—prioritize chaining captures for extra turns.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do 5-player and 6-player boards work?

Selecting 5 players generates a symmetrical 5-arm Pentagon Ludo board, and 6 players generates a 6-arm Hexagon Ludo board with full rules and safe stars.

Can I play with friends on separate phones?

Yes! Tap "Play Online", create a room, and share the 6-digit Room Code with your friends.
